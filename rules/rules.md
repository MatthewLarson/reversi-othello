## Reversi / Othello Rules

**Goal of the Game**\
The goal is simple:\
Finish the game with more discs showing your color than your opponent.\
One player uses black and the other uses white.\
**Players**

- 2 players
- One player is Black
- One player is White
  Black usually goes first.


**The Board**
Reversi is played on an 8 × 8 board with 64 spaces.
The game uses round discs that have:

- Black on one side
- White on the other side
  The discs are flipped during the game.


**Setup**
Place 4 discs in the middle of the board like this:

- 2 black discs
- 2 white discs
- Matching colors are placed diagonally from each other
  Example:
  White  Black
  Black  White

Black takes the first turn.


**How to Play**
On your turn, place one disc on an empty space.
But you cannot place a disc anywhere you want.
Your move must trap at least one of your opponent's discs.
To trap a disc:

1. Place your disc so one or more enemy discs are between your new disc and another disc of your color.
2. The trapped discs must be in a straight line.
3. Flip all of the trapped discs to your color.
   You may trap discs in more than one direction at the same time.


**You Can Flip Discs in 8 Directions**
Discs can be trapped:

- Up
- Down
- Left
- Right
- Diagonally in all 4 directions
  That means one move can sometimes flip several groups of discs at once.


**Example**
Imagine the board has:
Black  White  White  \_

If Black places a disc in the empty space:
Black  White  White  Black

The two white discs are trapped between black discs.
They flip to black:
Black  Black  Black  Black

**What Makes a Move Legal?**
A move is legal only if it flips at least one opponent disc.
You cannot place a disc:

- On an occupied space
- Somewhere that does not trap any opponent discs
  If a move flips nothing, you cannot play there.
  What If You Have No Moves?
  Sometimes you may have no legal move.
  If that happens:
  Your turn is skipped.
  Your opponent plays again.
  You do not lose just because you have to skip a turn.


**Corners Are Special**
The 4 corner spaces are very important.
Once a disc is placed in a corner, it can never be flipped.
Because of this, players often try to capture corners and avoid giving them away.

**When Does the Game End?**
The game ends when:

- The board is full, or
- Neither player has a legal move
  Then count all the discs.


**How to Win**
The player with the most discs showing their color wins.
For example:

- Black: 35
- White: 29
  Black wins.
  If both players have the same number of discs, the game is a tie.


**Simple Strategy Tips**
Try to Take Corners
Corners cannot be flipped, so they are very powerful.
Be Careful Near Corners
Spaces next to a corner can be dangerous.
Playing there may let your opponent take the corner.
Do Not Worry About Winning Early
Having the most discs in the middle of the game does not mean you will win.
A player can flip many discs near the end.
Think Ahead
Before you move, ask:
"What moves will this give my opponent?"
Sometimes flipping fewer discs now can help you win later.


**Quick Rule Summary**

1. Black goes first.
2. Place one disc on an empty space.
3. Your move must trap at least one enemy disc.
4. Flip every trapped disc to your color.
5. Players take turns.
6. If you have no legal move, skip your turn.
7. The game ends when no more moves can be made.
8. The player with the most discs wins.


**Remember**
Place. Trap. Flip. Control the board.
The player with the most discs at the end wins Reversi.
